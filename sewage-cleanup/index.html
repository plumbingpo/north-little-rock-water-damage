Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. When it happens in North Little Rock, North Little Rock Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
